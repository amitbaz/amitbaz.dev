# amitbaz.dev

A personal site that proves the claim "product engineer" rather than restating the CV: the reader arrives from the CV or LinkedIn, already holding the facts, and comes here for evidence of craft and of how Amit thinks.

## Language

**Reader**:
Anyone who clicks the link from the CV or LinkedIn — recruiter, sourcer, or hiring manager — deciding whether to take Amit further.
_Avoid_: User, visitor, recruiter (too narrow)

**Project**:
A piece of work Amit has done, shown by its outcome and his role in it. Past employer work cannot be exposed in detail, so Projects carry no attached documentation.
_Avoid_: Case study, portfolio item

**Process**:
How Amit thinks and works — a standalone destination on the site, not attached to any one Project. It has three parts: Principles, Agentic Workflow, and the Worked Example.
_Avoid_: Journal, blog, write-up, "process per project"

**Worked Example**:
This site's own build — brief, PRD, decisions, trade-offs — published as the one fully documented demonstration of the Process.
_Avoid_: Case study, meta-project

**Craft**:
The quality of the site itself — its design, interaction, and polish — treated as first-hand evidence of Amit's frontend ability. The page is not a container for the proof; it is part of the proof.
_Avoid_: Dynamic, modern, non-template

**Principles**:
The short, stated account of how Amit thinks and decides. The claims half of the Process; the Worked Example is the evidence half.
_Avoid_: Philosophy, values, manifesto

**Decision Story**:
A short account from past work — situation, the call Amit made, what happened. It may name the company and use any fact already on the public CV; internal specifics (people, customers, data, code, unreleased plans) stay out. The only form past employer work takes on the site beyond role summaries.
_Avoid_: Case study, war story, testimonial, anonymised story

**Agentic Workflow**:
How Amit works with AI coding agents: what he directs, what he delegates, what he never delegates, and the tooling he uses. The "how I work" part of the Process; the Worked Example is its proof.
_Avoid_: AI skills, prompt engineering, "using AI"

**Story**:
The destination that tells the pivot narrative — where Amit came from, what changed, where he is going — with the career history shown as a timeline inside it as evidence.
_Avoid_: About, bio, CV page, timeline (as a separate page)

**Release**:
A planned increment of the site after launch. The site ships in Releases; each one extends the Worked Example.
_Avoid_: Phase, milestone, sprint
