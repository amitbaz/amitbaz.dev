# The site positions Amit as a Product Engineer, with the pivot stated in the subline

Amit's career is in frontend engineering and the next roles he wants carry decision-making, planning, and system-design ownership (TPM / PM / product-engineer shaped). A headline that hedges between those reads as indecision; one that claims a PM or Staff title he hasn't held reads as inflation. We decided the headline is **Product Engineer** — the companies that own the term (PostHog, Ashby) define it by behaviour (research the problem, write the spec, build end-to-end), not by prior title, so it is credible from a frontend CV — and the subline carries the pivot and the agentic-workflow differentiator, in the spirit of "Frontend-rooted. Owns brief → PRD → system design → build, with agentic AI workflows." Full evidence in `docs/research/role-label.md`.

## Considered options

- **Technical Product Manager.** Tiny posting volume; postings gate on years held *as* a PM. Rejected as a self-label; kept as a stated *target* in the narrative.
- **Staff / Lead Frontend Engineer.** Best keyword recall, but ladders define Staff as cross-team scope not yet held, and Berlin/UK hiring managers explicitly filter title inflation. Rejected.
- **AI Engineer.** Postings skew Python/RAG; self-rebranding under it is a known red flag to UK hiring leads. AI/agentic stays in the subline, backed by the Worked Example.

## Consequences

- "Senior frontend" must still appear near the top for recruiter keyword search.
- PostHog/Ashby-style hirers expect full-stack; the site should make system-design and backend-adjacent work visible to pre-empt that filter.
- Of the personal sites surveyed that self-label "product engineer", 5 of 6 read as template; the crafted tier uses "design engineer" or a plain sentence about what they make (`docs/research/portfolio-references.md` §4). The label carries a template association that the site's Craft must visibly contradict.
- Only 2 of 25 surveyed sites document their own build. The Process page and Worked Example are a genuine differentiator, not table stakes.
