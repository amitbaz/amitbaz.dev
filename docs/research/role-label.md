# Role label for the landing-page headline

Research date: 2026-10-10. Scope: how a Berlin-based senior frontend engineer (5–8 yrs, informal PRD/system-design ownership, no PM/lead title, agentic-AI workflow as differentiator) should label themselves for remote Europe/UK/Israel roles, with SG/JP/TW as aspirational.

## 1. Recommendation

**Headline:** `Product Engineer`
**Subline:** `Frontend-rooted. Owns brief → PRD → system design → build, with agentic AI workflows.`

**Why:** The companies that own the term (PostHog, Ashby) define a product engineer as exactly the scope this person wants and already does informally — "research the problem, write product specs, build wireframes, and implement their solution end-to-end" — and neither requires a prior PM or lead title, so the label is credible on a frontend CV. It is also the fastest-growing of the candidate titles in the one market with measurable data (UK permanent postings 28 → 203 YoY) and is the house title at the remote-first EU/UK companies most likely to hire across borders (Ashby, Levity, Featurebase, Clera), while the subline keeps the "frontend/React/TypeScript" keywords recruiters actually search.

**Runner-up: `Staff / Lead Frontend Engineer`.** Highest raw keyword match for frontend recruiters and the GitLab/Oviva ladders describe Staff as "blends technical, product and design strategy" — close to the goal. It lost because the ladders also define Staff as cross-team technical leadership the person has never held by title, and Berlin/UK hiring managers in the 2026 Pragmatic Engineer survey explicitly complain about title-inflated CVs; self-promoting to Staff is the exact pattern they filter.

Do **not** put "AI Engineer" in the headline (see §4): UK postings under that title skew Python/Azure/RAG and a UK head of engineering is on record saying candidates "rebranding themselves as senior AI Engineers" get dug into and found hollow. Keep agentic/AI in the subline, backed by the site's own build log.

## 2. Label-by-label findings

### Product Engineer

1. **What companies mean.** PostHog careers page: "Owning products and features from beginning to end. This means originating ideas based on your intuition, talking to users… testing MVPs in production with real users… owning pricing" and "Product engineers at PostHog are full stack, so we expect you to ship and own the basic UX of your work using our design system" ([posthog.com/careers/product-engineer](https://posthog.com/careers/product-engineer)). PostHog's Product Engineer Handbook: "Unlike a traditionally defined full stack engineer, a product engineer's job goes beyond shipping code… They decide what to build. They own pricing, revenue, and user experience" ([posthog.com/blog/what-is-a-product-engineer](https://posthog.com/blog/what-is-a-product-engineer)). Ashby (Staff Product Engineer, UK, £138–224K, remote GB): "Product Managers (and Designers) build strategy, do customer research, and hand off problem briefs to Engineers. Engineers take on the rest: they research the problem, write product specs, build wireframes, and implement their solution end-to-end" ([ycombinator.com/companies/ashby/jobs/pLGE3S3](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ashby/jobs/pLGE3S3-staff-product-engineer-uk)).
2. **Demand.** UK (ITJobsWatch, 6 months to 10 Oct 2026): 203 permanent postings vs 28 (2025) and 38 (2024); median £80,000 vs £44,500 — the jump in median suggests the title shifted from hardware/manufacturing toward software ([itjobswatch.co.uk/jobs/uk/product engineer](https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/jobs/uk/product%20engineer.do)). Caveat: the co-occurring skill list still includes 5G/SD-WAN, so some telecom-hardware roles share the title. Remote-Europe examples found: Ashby Staff SWE Product Engineering EU (posted 27 Aug 2026, [jobicy](https://jobicy.com/jobs/151856-staff-software-engineer-product-engineering-eu-2.md)), Levity "Product Engineer, Remote (Europe)" ([levityai.notion.site](https://levityai.notion.site/Product-Engineer-3509127bc66580e591d4ecb1ea6129ce)), Featurebase Remote Europe ([featurebase.app/careers/product-engineer](https://www.featurebase.app/careers/product-engineer)), Clera "Product Engineer (Full-Stack)" Berlin on-site ([ziprecruiter.de](https://www.ziprecruiter.de/jobs/570606818-product-engineer-full-stack-bei-clera)). No Germany-wide or Israel count available.
3. **Recruiter / HM interpretation.** Ashby's co-founder/VP Eng frames the title as a reaction to engineers becoming "a JIRA jockey" and wants "an environment that is optimistic about what engineers can own" (same YC posting). PostHog hires its PMs from "ex-founder or ex-product engineer" and says PMs "coach product engineers on how to do product" ([posthog.com/careers/product-manager-(ex-founder-or-ex-product-engineer)](https://posthog.com/careers/product-manager-(ex-founder-or-ex-product-engineer))) — i.e. the companies using the term treat product engineering as the on-ramp to product ownership. PostHog's handbook also states "You can't be a truly effective product engineer without AI assistance anymore… table stakes" ([posthog.com/product-engineer/skills](https://posthog.com/product-engineer/skills)).
4. **Fit.** Strong. Every definition above is about behaviour (talk to users, write specs, own outcomes), not prior title. Filter-out risk: PostHog/Ashby say "full stack"; a purely frontend CV may be screened for backend depth at those two. Mitigation is the "frontend-rooted" subline plus visible backend/system-design work on the site. Secondary risk: German-market and UK job-board ambiguity with mechanical "Product Engineer" (see §3).

### Technical Product Manager

1. **Meaning.** GitLab PM job family: PMs "are decision-makers accountable for identifying where their group should invest engineering and design capacity, and owning the outcomes" and must "understand how software is built, packaged, deployed and operated" ([handbook.gitlab.com/job-families/product/product-manager](https://handbook.gitlab.com/job-families/product/product-manager/)). GitLab has no separate TPM family. Berlin postings: IOTA Foundation wants "3+ years in technical product management" and 8 years total; Zenjob accepts an engineering degree "or several years as a technical PM" ([arbeitnow IOTA](https://arbeitnow.com/jobs/companies/iota-foundation/technical-product-manager-twin-all-genders-berlin-170460), [arbeitnow Zenjob](https://arbeitnow.com/jobs/companies/zenjob/technical-product-manager-berlin-313668)). London: Bloomberg "8+ years in technical product management" ([itjobboard.co.uk](https://www.itjobboard.co.uk/job/16700435/strategic-technical-product-manager-data-manufacturing-infra/)); Intellect Group says the role "suits people who are moving into product management" from engineering ([itjobswatch.co.uk](https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/jv/Intellect-Group/Product-Manager-Job-London-UK-4xhtm8)).
2. **Demand.** UK: 22 permanent postings (6 months to Oct 2026) vs 15 and 62; rank fell 131 places YoY; median £100,000 but only 13 salaries quoted ([itjobswatch TPM](https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/jobs/uk/technical%20product%20manager.do)). Tiny volume.
3. **Interpretation.** No first-party recruiter commentary found specific to TPM. GitLab's ladder treats "product management experience" as the baseline, not engineering experience.
4. **Fit.** Weak as a headline. Most postings gate on years *as* a PM; the person has none by title. Claiming it invites the "CVs are high-quality, but the people behind them are not" reaction a Berlin EM gives in the Pragmatic Engineer survey ([newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/tech-jobs-market-in-2026-part-3-hiring)). Use TPM as a *target* in the narrative, not a self-label.

### Staff / Lead Frontend Engineer

1. **Meaning.** GitLab: "A staff engineer generally operates at a team level scope, serving as the technical leader for one or more of their team's domains" and "Blends technical, product and design strategy" ([handbook.gitlab.com …/frontend/staff](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/engineering/careers/matrix/development/dev/frontend/staff/)). Oviva (Germany, Apr 2026): "Unlike a Senior Engineer who masters a specific domain, you will provide the technical vision and architectural guardrails for several domains… Act as a technical lead on cross-team projects… championing… the strategic use of LLMs and AI-driven development practices" ([partechpartners Oviva](https://portfoliojobs.partechpartners.com/companies/oviva/jobs/75490524-staff-frontend-engineer)).
2. **Demand.** ITJobsWatch has no "Staff Engineer" page (404). Proxy: England "Front-End Developer" 219 postings (vs 133 / 382), median £65,000; "Senior Front-End Developer" England is ~82 postings with React/TypeScript/Mentoring as top skills and Claude Code already appearing as a listed skill ([front-end developer](https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/jobs/england/front-end%20developer.do), [senior front-end developer](https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/jobs/england/senior%20front-end%20developer.do)). Frontend postings are down 43% on 2024.
3. **Interpretation.** Ladders are explicit that Staff is a *scope* (multi-team), not a seniority bump.
4. **Fit.** "Senior Frontend Engineer" is honest and the highest-recall keyword; "Staff/Lead" is not held and reads as inflation. Keep "senior frontend" in the subline for search, not as the headline claim.

### Forward Deployed Engineer

1. **Meaning.** Palantir: "your responsibilities look similar to those of a startup CTO: you'll work in small teams with minimal supervision and own end-to-end execution of high stakes projects"; works "externally with our customers, often on site" ([jobs.lever.co/palantir](https://jobs.lever.co/palantir/98b81271-9e15-40b7-8dce-0c62f24702f9)). Anthropic (London, £225–255K, Sep 2026): "Deliver technical artifacts for customers like MCP servers, sub-agents, and agent skills"; requires "4+ years of experience in a technical, customer facing role", "Production experience with LLMs including advanced prompt engineering, agent development, evaluation", Python, "Travel frequently (25-50%)"; "We do sponsor visas!" ([jobs.accel.com Anthropic FDE](https://jobs.accel.com/companies/anthropic/jobs/93102649-forward-deployed-engineer)). OpenAI (London, hybrid 3 days, travel up to 50%): "own discovery, technical scoping, system design, build, and production rollout"; "5+ years of engineering or technical deployment experience" ([agentic-engineering-jobs.com OpenAI FDE](https://agentic-engineering-jobs.com/jobs/openai-forward-deployed-engineer-london-belz7K)).
2. **Demand.** No ITJobsWatch page (404) — too new/small for UK aggregate stats. Postings found are London hybrid at frontier labs and Palantir on-site in EU offices; not remote.
3. **Interpretation.** Term is owned by Palantir; frontier labs copy it verbatim. Both define it as customer-embedded delivery.
4. **Fit.** The *responsibilities* (discovery, scoping, system design, build) match the goal almost exactly, but the gates are customer-facing experience, production-LLM work, Python, and 25–50% travel. Not a self-label; a legitimate target role to name on the site.

### AI Engineer

1. **Meaning.** Term coined by swyx (Jun 2023): sits to the right of "the API line", building atop foundation-model APIs rather than training them ([latent.space/p/ai-engineer](https://www.latent.space/p/ai-engineer)). UK postings: oho group "Agentic AI Engineer" asks for "building agents that handle multi-step tasks and integrating models with tools and APIs" ([oho-group](https://oho-group.sites.sourceflow.co.uk/jobs/agentic-ai-engineer/)).
2. **Demand.** Highest growth of any label: UK permanent postings 806 (vs 91 / 46), rank 265 (from 576), median £87,500 ([itjobswatch AI Engineer](https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/jobs/uk/artificial%20intelligence%20engineer.do)). LinkedIn Jobs on the Rise Jan 2026: #1 UK, #2 Germany ([euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/01/08/half-of-europeans-seek-a-new-job-in-2026-but-most-feel-unprepared-to-find-one)). But the UK skill mix is Python 55%, Azure 43%, LLM 36%, RAG 21% — a backend/ML profile, not frontend.
3. **Interpretation.** UK head of engineering, 2026: "Lots of people are rebranding themselves as senior AI Engineers… when digging deeper there is little substance" ([pragmaticengineer part 3](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/tech-jobs-market-in-2026-part-3-hiring)).
4. **Fit.** Poor as headline: *using* agentic coding tools is not what "AI Engineer" postings mean (they mean building LLM products). A frontend CV under this title is filtered on Python/RAG and reads as the rebrand HMs distrust. Keep in subline.

### Design Engineer

1. **Meaning.** Vercel: "Design Engineer is a new role that is gaining popularity… The Design Engineer will sketch in Figma or code a solution, socialize the change, incorporate feedback, then ship it"; focus on "polished interactions, no dropped frames… accessibility" ([vercel.com/blog/design-engineering-at-vercel](https://vercel.com/blog/design-engineering-at-vercel)). Ashby (UK, remote): "Design Engineer at Ashby isn't just a Frontend Engineer with new branding, nor is it a Designer vibe coding prototypes… This role truly expects you to design and code" ([builtinlondon Ashby](https://builtinlondon.uk/job/design-engineer-uk/9930462)). Linear "Design Engineer (Web & Brand)", Europe-eligible: requires "an eye for good design… portfolio showing creative coding", React/TypeScript ([jobs.accel.com Linear](https://jobs.accel.com/companies/linear/jobs/34559943-design-engineer-web-brand)).
2. **Demand.** ITJobsWatch "Design Engineer" shows 577 UK postings but the skill list is AutoCAD 14%, CAD 13%, HNC/HND, Firmware, C — overwhelmingly mechanical/electronic ([itjobswatch Design Engineer](https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/jobs/uk/design%20engineer.do)). Software design-engineer volume is unmeasurable from aggregates and anecdotally small (Vercel, Linear, Ashby).
3. **Interpretation.** Owners (Vercel, Ashby) define it by design craft + portfolio, and Ashby explicitly rejects "frontend engineer with new branding".
4. **Fit.** Only if there is a visual-design portfolio. Otherwise it is exactly the rebrand Ashby warns against. Not recommended.

### Engineering Manager

1. **Meaning.** GitLab: "Demonstrated progressive experience in a leadership role with current technical experience" ([handbook.gitlab.com EM](https://handbook.gitlab.com/job-families/engineering/development/management/engineering-manager/)). Berlin: Voize "you are required to have people management experience" ([choppingblock voize](https://www.choppingblock.ai/jobs/engineering-manager-m-f-d-at-voize)); N26 asks for "experience growing, coaching, mentoring and providing feedback to team members including having had difficult conversations" ([n26.com](https://n26.com/en-eu/careers/positions/6442745)).
2. **Demand.** UK: 814 postings (vs 260 / 339), median £85,000 ([itjobswatch EM](https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/jobs/uk/engineering%20manager.do)). Plentiful, but people-management-gated.
3./4. **Fit.** Not credible without reports. The person wants decision/planning ownership plus hands-on building; EM postings trade the latter away. Skip.

### Founding Engineer

1. **Meaning.** Berlin (Scripe): "shaping core product areas end-to-end, influencing architecture and engineering culture" ([arbeitnow Scripe](https://www.arbeitnow.com/jobs/companies/scripe/founding-engineer-berlin-51919)); Clera Berlin: on-site, "must be eligible to work in Germany without employer visa sponsorship" ([ziprecruiter.de Clera](https://www.ziprecruiter.de/jobs/554558517-founding-engineer-bei-clera)).
2. **Demand.** No ITJobsWatch page. Postings are mostly on-site, compensation vague, "remote Europe" the exception.
3./4. **Fit.** It describes a *stage of company*, not a skill set; nobody self-labels as founding engineer before being one. Scope matches the goal, so list it as a target, not a headline.

### Full-Stack Product Engineer

Variant of Product Engineer used by Clera, Jobgether and others. Adding "full-stack" to a frontend CV invites a backend-depth screen that "frontend-rooted" avoids. Use "Product Engineer" and let the body prove stack breadth.

## 3. Market notes

**Europe remote / UK.** Remote-first companies hiring across Europe/UK use "Product Engineer" or "Design Engineer" house titles (Ashby, Linear, Levity, Featurebase); corporates use "Senior/Staff Frontend Engineer". UK sponsorship: Skilled Worker general threshold £41,700; SOC 2134 "Programmers and software development professionals" (incl. "Software developers") standard going rate £54,700, lower £40,000; SOC 2133 (architects/systems designers) £54,900 ([gov.uk going rates](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/skilled-worker-visa-going-rates-for-eligible-occupations/skilled-worker-visa-going-rates-for-eligible-occupation-codes)). A "Product Engineer" title is still classed under 2134 for sponsorship, so the label has no visa downside. Frontend-titled UK postings fell 43% from 2024; AI-titled rose ~9x — the subline must carry the AI signal.

**Israel.** Israel Innovation Authority 2026 report: only 62% of private Israeli tech-company employees are based in Israel (69% in 2019), with growth abroad in R&D, sales, support and management ([innovationisrael.org.il 2026 report](https://innovationisrael.org.il/en/press_release/israeli-high-tech-report-2026/)) — Israeli companies do hire R&D remotely, which fits a Berlin-based candidate with an Israeli passport. No title-frequency data found; postings seen use plain "Full-Stack"/"Frontend Engineer" ([titandxp](https://titandxp.com/careers-position/full-stack-developer/)). "Product Engineer" is not established vocabulary there; the subline's "frontend" carries the search.

**Germany / Berlin.** English titles dominate Berlin tech postings, with "(m/w/d)" appended; the Arbeitsagentur classifies "Software Engineer (m/w/d)" under "Berufsbezeichnung: Softwareentwickler/in" ([arbeitsagentur.de](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/jobsuche/jobdetail/18988-ppcC5vH9-S)). Warning: "Product Engineer"/"Produktingenieur" in German boards is frequently a mechanical role requiring a Maschinenbau degree ([arbeitsagentur Product Engineer (m/w/d)](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/jobsuche/jobdetail/17350-k59209.3263-S), [Capgemini](https://careers.capgemini.com/job/Sant-Esteve-Sesrovires-Product-Engineer-with-German/1286080801)). Berlin startups (Clera, Scripe) do use it in the software sense. Mitigation: subline mentions frontend/TypeScript so keyword filters disambiguate. Berlin EMs cited in the Pragmatic Engineer survey are notably sceptical of polished-but-hollow CVs; a self-documenting build log is the right antidote.

**Singapore / Japan / Taiwan.** Taiwan Gold Card digital field: NT$160,000/month recent salary, or 8 years' digital-economy experience, or top-500 PhD; reviewed by the Ministry of Digital Affairs ([goldcard.nat.gov.tw](https://goldcard.nat.gov.tw/en/), [smartcity.org.tw summary](https://en.smartcity.org.tw/index.php/en-us/taiwangoldcard)) — title is irrelevant, salary/experience evidence is what counts. Japan: foreigner-friendly firms use "Software Engineer, Frontend" / "Frontend Engineer"; PayPay requires no Japanese, Merpay wants B2 in either language ([japan-dev Merpay](https://japan-dev.com/jobs/merpay/merpay-software-engineer-frontend-so9dwp), [japan-dev PayPay](https://japan-dev.com/jobs/paypay/paypay-frontend-engineer-el36re?lang=jp)). Singapore: titles are conventional ("Principal Software Engineer (Team Lead)"); employers prioritise citizens/PRs and EP sponsorship is limited (anecdotal, [LinkedIn post](https://tw.linkedin.com/in/dino-liu-14aa90133)). No primary source found for "Product Engineer" usage in any of the three; conventional "Frontend Engineer" keywords matter more there.

## 4. Where AI/agentic belongs: subline, not title

- The owners of "Product Engineer" already fold AI into the role: PostHog — "learning how to use AI effectively is table stakes for a product engineer" ([posthog.com/product-engineer/skills](https://posthog.com/product-engineer/skills)); Vercel's 2026 Design Engineer posting mentions using "coding agents… to explore and build ideas quickly" ([jobs.generalcatalyst.com Vercel](https://jobs.generalcatalyst.com/companies/vercel/jobs/93299295-design-engineer)); Oviva's Staff Frontend posting lists "AI-driven development practices". So AI-workflow fluency is an *expected attribute* of the engineering title, not a separate title.
- Putting "AI" in the title maps you onto the AI Engineer skill profile (Python/Azure/RAG) you don't match, and onto the "rebranding as AI Engineer" distrust quoted in §2 ([pragmaticengineer](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/tech-jobs-market-in-2026-part-3-hiring)).
- "Claude Code" already appears as a named skill in UK Senior Front-End postings ([itjobswatch senior front-end](https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/jobs/england/senior%20front-end%20developer.do)), so naming the tool in the subline/body hits a real keyword without changing the title.
- Evidence beats adjectives: the site's brief → PRD → system design → implementation log is the proof hiring managers say they can't get from CVs. Link it from the subline.
